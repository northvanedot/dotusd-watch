Before it exists: what referendum 1944 does on enactment

  1. Creates the asset on Asset Hub: id 7873, six decimals, sufficient.
  2. Creates the PSM: USDT in, dotUSD out at 1:1, ceiling 10,000,000, minting fee 0, redemption fee 0.01%.
  3. Registers dotUSD on the People chain, so it can be held there.
  4. Seeds a DOT/dotUSD pool from the treasury: mints 2,500,000.02 dotUSD from USDT and pairs 2,500,000 of it with 2,853,229.86 DOT.
  5. Creates a bearer-coin instance for dotUSD on the People chain.
